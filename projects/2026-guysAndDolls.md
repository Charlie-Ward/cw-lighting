Meet Nathan Detroit, a gambler, trying to set up New York’s biggest Craps dice game whilst the cops are right on his tail, and Sky Masterson, who made a bet that he can win the heart of missionary Sarah Brown. This raucously exciting show, steeped in the gangster underbelly of 1950s New York City, flits between the cafés of Havana and the sewers of NYC. Performed by the Richard Burton Company, the rom-com classic will have you singing iconic numbers like 'I’ve Never Been in Love Before', 'Sit Down, Your Rockin’ the Boat' and 'Luck Be a Lady'.
Guys and Dolls has become one of the greatest American musicals of all time!
